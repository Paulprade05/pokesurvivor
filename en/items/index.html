PokéSurvivor held items

During a run you can find held items in chests and by defeating bosses. Each one is common, rare, epic or legendary (the rarer, the stronger). You hold one; once your 4 moves are fused you can hold two. Hold the same one for 10 minutes and it evolves: much stronger and with its own passive.

  • LeftoversRegenerate 1.3% of your HP per second. Evolves into Eternal Leftovers: Below 30% HP, regeneration from your items triples.
  • Choice Band+34% damage. You can't switch moves. Evolves into Champion Band: No longer locks you into one move.
  • Quick Claw-17% cooldown on all your moves. Evolves into Lightning Claw: Every 12 s, your current move recharges instantly.
  • Focus SashA hit that would knock you out leaves you at 1 HP (every 71 s). Evolves into Valor Sash: Al salvarte, recuperas un 21 % de vida and lanzas una onda que daña and empuja.
  • Shell BellRecover 3.4% of the damage you deal (capped per second). Evolves into Tide Bell: Every 6 s, a water wave damages enemies around you and heals you with it.
  • Amulet Coin+43% PokéCoins at the end of the run. Evolves into Golden Coin: It shines so bright it also gives you extra experience.
  • Lucky Egg+26% experience. Evolves into Golden Egg: Every 141 s, you instantly gain half a level of experience.
  • Life Orb+26% damage. Each attack costs a bit of HP. Evolves into Pure Life Orb: Ya no te quita vida and haces un +13 % de daño a jefes and legendarios.
  • Choice Scarf+30% speed. Evolves into Blizzard Scarf: While running you leave an icy trail that slows enemies by 34%.
  • Rocky HelmetFoes that hit you in melee take damage. Evolves into Spike Helmet: Every hit you take releases a wave of spikes around you (at most every 2 s).
  • Assault Vest-17% damage taken. Evolves into Titan Vest: Any single hit above 15% of your HP has the excess halved.
  • Bright PowderDodge 6.8% of hits. Evolves into Star Powder: When you dodge, you dazzle nearby enemies: they are slowed by 51% for 2 s.
  • Liechi BerryBelow half HP, +30% damage. Evolves into Ripe Liechi Berry: Once a minute, the first time you drop below half HP you recover 17%.
  • Oran BerryWhen you drop below 35% HP, you recover 21% (recharges in 59 s). Evolves into Giant Oran Berry: When you eat it, you also gain a barrier of 17% of your HP.
  • Metal Coat+13% max HP. Evolves into Steel Armor: Every 12 s you gain a barrier of 10% of your HP.
  • Eviolite-13% damage taken. +8.5% damage. Full effect while your Pokémon can still evolve; half otherwise. Evolves into Eternal Eviolite: Full effect even if your Pokémon can no longer evolve.
  • Toxic OrbYour hits poison 13% of the time. +13% damage to poisoned enemies. Evolves into Black Toxic Orb: Every 4 s, a toxic cloud poisons nearby enemies.
  • Soothe BellHealing heals you 30% more. Evolves into Serene Bell: Overhealing is stored as a barrier (up to 17% of your HP).
  • White Herb+6.8% speed. Sticky traps, water and ice don't slow you down. Evolves into Pure Herb: When hit, +26% speed for 3 s.
  • Air Balloon+8.5% speed. You float over water and lava. Evolves into Hot-Air Balloon: Every 10 s you dive down: a wave damages and pushes back enemies around you.
  • Soul Dew+17% damage with moves of your types. Evolves into Eternal Dew: Moves of your types get +8.5% critical hit chance.
  • Weakness PolicyWhen hit by a super-effective attack, +21% damage for 10 s (stacks twice). Evolves into Total Policy: Any strong hit (10% of your HP or more) also triggers it.
  • SnowballEnemies that hit you in melee are slowed by 43% for 2 s. Evolves into Avalanche: Every 8 s, a blizzard slows nearby enemies by 68% for 2 s.
  • Sticky BarbEvery second, damages enemies right next to you. Evolves into Cursed Barbs: Its aura is 50% larger and poisons.
  • Macho Brace+51% experience. -12% speed. Evolves into Champion Brace: No longer slows you down.
  • Expert Belt+21% damage with super-effective hits. Evolves into Master Belt: Your not-very-effective hits deal neutral damage.
